A card-expiry bug, from ticket to Linear issue

Figma Merch Store reports that shoppers' cards are rejected as expired. The real cause is a bug planted in payment deploy v1.4.0, where the expiry check changed from > to >=. IDs, times and counts are illustrative.

Where the models are called

Agents with tools run only in the tech-issue lane: the two analysts, first in parallel and then on each other's questions. Every other model step is a single call with structured output, and plain lookups and decisions stay in code.
