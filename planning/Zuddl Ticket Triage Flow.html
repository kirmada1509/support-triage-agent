Zuddl Ticket Triage Flow
Zuddl · internal support harness

Ticket triage flow, traced with Figma as the customer

Figma runs its Config events on Zuddl. When their events team opens a ticket in Pylon, a webhook hands it to the agent, which categorizes it with Jev (TypeSafe AI's typed decision model) and either answers on its own or sends it to the right layer: product support, tech support, or engineering.
